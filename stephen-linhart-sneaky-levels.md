## Stephen Linhart's Sneaky Levels

In 1996, [Stephen Linhart](https://slinhart.com/) and Doug Hewitt crafted 30 very tough and interesting levels for Lode Runner. Most of these levels are very intellectual. Some may seem to be impossible, but they have all been done by two or more people. These levels were created by Stephen Linhart and Doug Hewitt. Have fun!

**Source:** [a newsgroup from 1997](https://info-mac.org/viewtopic.php?t=5066).
